<div class="utter-strip">
  <div>
    <strong>Push-to-talk</strong>
    <p>Hold the assistant key and speak, or hold the dictation key to type what you say into any field.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Desktop actions</strong>
    <p>Open apps and websites, focus and close windows, switch workspaces, control media, press app shortcuts.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Rules first</strong>
    <p>Most commands match fast, predictable rules. A small local model only chooses among prepared actions. It never invents commands.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Offline</strong>
    <p>Speech recognition, AI models and screenshots all stay on your machine. No account, no telemetry.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Frees your GPU</strong>
    <p>Say "go to sleep", or just stop using it for a while, and the models unload. Hold a key to wake it in a fraction of a second.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Two modes, two keys" icon="seti:audio">
    Hold the **assistant key** (Insert by default) to run a command. Hold the **dictation key** (F13 by default) to type what you say. Each mode has its own sound and waveform colour.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understands context" icon="laptop">
    Utter knows which app is focused and what is on screen. It uses accessibility information first and takes a screenshot only as a last resort.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-app actions you can edit" icon="pen">
    More than 100 app profiles with their shortcuts. Change any key combination from the settings app or a YAML file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Safe by default" icon="approve-check">
    Terminal commands and raw input stay off until you turn them on. Important actions ask first. Screen text can select, but never author, an action.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
